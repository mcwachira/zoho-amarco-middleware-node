# Integration contract evidence register

This register separates published interface facts from locally implemented
behavior. A documented contract is not proof that the customer's tenant,
enrollment, software version, or device behaves accordingly.

and live rate-limit behavior are not verified.

## Cross-system decisions

- **Legal/business decision:** no verified legal or customer policy establishes whether the separate Tremol receipt and eTIMS reporting operation must run first. VSCU's internal sales-record dependency is not a rule for ordering the separate hardware receipt. The combined production flow remains blocked.
- **Implementation against synthetic contracts:** simulator and mock eTIMS tests validate internal classification, persistence, and recovery only. They do not claim Zoho, KRA, ZFP Lab, or physical-device conformance.
- **External acceptance required:** obtain current taxpayer-specific KRA specifications and enrollment, certified VSCU/OSCU environment details, vendor-supported G03/ZFP versions, command/result fixtures, and signed legal/business sequence approval before enabling live operations.
