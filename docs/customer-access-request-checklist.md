# Customer access request checklist

Use this before scheduling an on-site visit. Send the request through the
customer's approved channel, agree who will supervise access, and record only
the fact that access was granted or denied. Do not request passwords, access
tokens, device passwords, encryption keys, or full database connection strings
by email, chat, ticket, or this worksheet. Credentials must be provisioned
directly to the approved account or service by the responsible administrator.

For the visit sequence and printable evidence sheet, see the
[office acceptance runbook](customer-office-acceptance-runbook.md). For current
configuration and operational limits, see [operations and deployment](operations-and-deployment.md).

## Access request wording

Use plain language and ask for access through the approved customer process:

> “For this visit, please provide a named, time-limited account through your
> approved access method. We need only the read access required to confirm the
> middleware host, PostgreSQL target, and installed LabServer version. If an
> administrative action becomes necessary, we will pause and ask the named
> administrator to supervise that specific change. Please provision any
> credentials directly through your approved secret-management process; do not
> send passwords or tokens to us in chat or email.”

Do not ask the customer to bypass access controls, share an administrator
password, disable MFA, or widen a firewall rule as a shortcut.
